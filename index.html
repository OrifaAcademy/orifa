About

A designer, a trainer, and a curious mind in AI.

I trained as a fashion designer and now work across three disciplines that keep informing one another: creative design, digital training, and generative AI.

My practice sits at the point where a garment sketch, a brand system and a machine-generated image can all describe the same idea. Through Orifa, the training platform I'm building, I help other creatives find that same intersection in their own work — turning craft into a clear, confident professional identity.

My Approach

Design isn't a finish. It's a process I build with you.

I don't treat design as a picture at the end of a brief. It starts as an idea, gets shaped through iteration, and becomes an experience — a garment, a brand, a course — that someone else can actually use and recognize.

That's the same thinking I bring to teaching: a concept only counts once it's been built, tested and made someone's own.
